# 21 Questions

A yes-or-no guessing game in a single file. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install or server needed.

## Modes

- **You guess my word (1 player).** The computer secretly picks an animal, food, object, vehicle, place or something from nature. Type any yes/no question ("Does it have four legs?", "Is it bigger than a breadbox?") and it answers. Up to 3 hints: the category, then a strong clue, then the first letter and length.
- **I guess your word (1 player).** You think of something, and the computer asks questions and guesses. It picks whichever question splits the remaining possibilities most evenly. If you stump it, you can teach it the answer (saved in your browser).
- **Party mode (2 to 8 players, pass-and-play).** Each round one player types a secret word while the others look away. Then the others take turns asking, out loud or typed, and the thinker taps Yes / No / Sometimes / Don't know and sets how hot the guessers are. A correct guess scores 5 points plus 1 per question left. If nobody gets it, the thinker scores 15.

## What makes it fun

- 21 marquee bulbs track your questions: green for yes, red for no, blue for sometimes.
- A closeness bar. In solo mode it measures how many possibilities your answers have ruled out. In party mode the thinker sets it.
- Confetti and cheers on every yes, on finding the category, and when you get warm, hot or close. Plus a reacting mascot and sound effects (toggle in the header).
