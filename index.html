Think of something. Then ask.

Yes-or-no questions only. You get 21 of them. Guess it before the bulbs run out.

How it works

  • The secret can be an animal, food, object, vehicle, place or something from nature.
  • Every question uses one of the 21 bulbs. Green means yes, red means no, blue means sometimes.
  • The closeness bar fills as you rule things out. Hit a yes on the category and you get a cheer.
  • A wrong guess also uses a bulb, so be brave but not reckless.
  • Online party: every friend uses their own phone. They need a Claude account and an invite to this game as an Editor.
